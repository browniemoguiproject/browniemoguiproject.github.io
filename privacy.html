This policy describes data processing in the Game Sentinel Android application and the external services used for its features and advertising.

2. App features and data on your device

Game Sentinel does not require an account. The publisher does not operate a server that receives or stores users' watched-game lists, and the app does not integrate Firebase Analytics.

The app stores watched games, notification settings, price thresholds, check results, the selected store country, language and audio preferences locally on your device. It also stores images and technical data required for its features, including information used to prevent duplicate notifications. Cached covers can be displayed offline.

These settings are not sent to a publisher-operated server as an archive of your watched games. However, searches and monitoring send search terms or game identifiers and titles to external services, together with the store country and language where required by the source.

The app does not request access to contacts, the camera, microphone or GPS location. The store country is selected by the user and does not represent GPS location tracking.

3. Requests to external services

To retrieve information, prices, covers and updates, the app connects directly from your device to third-party services, including Steam and its image distribution networks, EGData for Epic Games Store data, and other third-party sites used as information and update sources.

When your device makes a request, the recipient receives your IP address and the necessary request parameters and may process technical connection information. Service operators may retain logs under their own practices and privacy policies. These requests provide search results and monitor the games and notifications you select, including in the background.

Links to Steam, Epic Games Store and other sites open in your browser or an available external app. Accounts, cookies, purchases and other data on those services are governed by their respective policies. Game Sentinel does not process purchases or receive credentials for those accounts.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show advertisements. Although the publisher does not maintain a user database on its own server, the advertising service involves data processing.

According to Google's documentation, the SDK collects and shares IP addresses, interaction information, diagnostic data, and device or account identifiers, depending on the configuration and available features. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. This data is used for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps using its services.

The app integrates Google User Messaging Platform to manage advertising choices. A consent message is shown where required. When available, the advertising privacy options in the app's information panel let you review your choices.

Consent to personalization may be withdrawn without affecting processing already performed before withdrawal. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all technical data processing by the advertising service. Advertising identifiers can also be managed through Android settings.

5. Purposes and grounds for processing

Local data and requests to information sources are used for the features you request: browsing the catalogue, searching, comparing prices, watching games and receiving notifications. The publisher does not use this data to build advertising profiles of users.

Where applicable, processing necessary to provide the requested features is based on providing the service; processing for security and abuse prevention is based on legitimate interests, subject to users' rights. Consent is requested for advertising processing and access to or storage of device information where required by applicable law. External providers describe their own grounds for processing in their policies.

If you contact the publisher, your email address and the information you voluntarily provide are processed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device while retained by the app. You can remove watched games, disable individual notifications, and delete all local data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the app. Clearing data resets preferences and removes cached covers.

Communications sent to the publisher are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. Retention periods for data processed by external providers depend on their policies. Deleting local data does not automatically delete information already processed by those providers.

7. Notifications and user choices

For individual watched games, users can enable notifications about price changes that meet their selected threshold, repack availability or updates, new patches, and newly released Steam DLC. Notifications can also be disabled in Android settings. Checks run on the phone; the publisher does not operate a push-notification server.

8. External providers and transfers

External providers may process data outside your country. Refer to their policies for processing locations, safeguards and any applicable transfer mechanisms. The publisher does not control the logs or systems of external sites.

9. Rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing and exercise rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw consent where applicable. Requests can be sent to gamesentinel@protonmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

The publisher can act on data it holds, such as communications received. Data held only on your phone can be managed directly by you. For data independently processed by an external provider, use the contacts and tools described in that provider's policy.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if app features, services or processing practices change. The date at the top indicates the most recent update.
