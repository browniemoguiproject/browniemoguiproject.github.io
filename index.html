Inside the app

Explore. Watch. Stay informed.

01 / EXPLORE

Find your game

Search by title, year and letter. Game pages with covers, descriptions, genres and release dates.

02 / COMPARE

Check the price

Store prices when available, for your selected country and in the currency returned by the store. Links to GoCDKeys help you explore key offers.

03 / WATCH

Choose your notifications

Save watched games and enable price alerts, newly released Steam DLC, PC game page availability on GoCDKeys, repack availability, or new patches when available.

Checks run on your device. Data availability and notification timing also depend on external sources and Android settings.
